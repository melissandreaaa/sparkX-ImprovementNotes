Information Architecture Principle

Prinsip Keputusan: The Golden Rule Pemisahan Menu

Model mental yang jelas sehingga HR Admin & Super Admin selalu tahu di mana mencari atau mengubah suatu data.

Master Data

"Kata Benda" (The Nouns)
Menjawab: Siapa, Apa, Di Mana & Klasifikasi
  • Data fondasi & kamus referensi yang dipakai ulang lintas modul.
  • Companies, Org Hierarchy, Business Structure, Reporting Lines.
  • Cost Centers, GL Accounts & WBS Projects.
  • Working Sites & Meeting Rooms (aset lokasi fisik).
  • Positions, Layers/PS Levels & Job Classifications.
  • Currencies, Banks, Geo Data, Profile & Document Lookups.

Configuration

"Kata Kerja & Aturan" (The Verbs)
Menjawab: Bagaimana sistem menghitung & menyetujui
  • Aturan perilaku, plafon, alur persetujuan & tata kelola.
  • Workflow & Approval Matrix lintas modul.
  • Notifications, AI & Automation, Roles & Ownership (RBAC).
  • Leave & Holiday Policies, Time & Attendance, Overtime Rules.
  • Claim & Expenses Caps, Medical TCare Matrix, Travel Per Diem.
  • Profile Verification, Onboarding/Offboarding & Document Rules.

HR Operations

"Operasional Harian" (Live Records)
Menjawab: Transaksi & direktori karyawan aktif
  • Data operasional yang berubah setiap hari oleh aktivitas HR.
  • Employee Directory (dipindahkan dari Master Data).
  • Organization Chart (tersinkron dengan Reporting Lines).
  • Profile Requests & Pending Approvals.
  • Onboarding & Offboarding Active Checklists.
  • Tetap terhubung 1 klik dari Setup Health di Admin Hub.

Page 8 · Config: Time & Attendance

Time & Attendance Gaps (Benchmarked)

Missing policy controls & unclear section naming compared to standard HRIS.

Key Findings & Fixes

  • Rename & Org Default: Rename Attendance Sites → Attendance Configuration; clarify/add Organization defaults.
  • Share Work Sites: Add Copy/Save Location across companies in the same building.
  • Late Grace Period: Add tolerance minutes for late clock-in.
  • Multiple Shifts: Support morning / night / rotational shift templates.
  • WFH Limit & Back-Dated Window: Add WFH day quota + max back-dated days (e.g. 7 days) for Manual Time Entry.

Page 9 · Config: Overtime

Overtime Gaps & Holiday/Rate Integration

Need minimum OT duration, revert option, and clear link to Work Week & Holidays.

Key Findings & Fixes

  • Revert to Standard Types: Add option to reset back after clicking Edit the standard types.
  • Minimum OT Duration: Prevent <30 min stays from being claimed as paid OT.
  • Holiday / Weekend OT: Integrate with Work Week & Holidays to detect public holiday OT & apply separate rules/rates.
  • "Overtime rates" Link: Guide says "not configured here"—link directly to where it lives (or unify).
